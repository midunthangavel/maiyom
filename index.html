01The product

The app isn’t
the task.

Everyday digital life is scattered across apps, services and repeated little actions. Phonon is being built to meet people at the level of what they want done.

Explain the outcome in natural language. Phonon aims to understand the request, coordinate supported services and help move the task forward—while making the next step clear.

Under exploration

A quieter way
to reach Phonon.

Maiyom is exploring a compact, portable voice interface—a dedicated access point to a personal AI environment, not another general-purpose smartphone.

The concept could take shape as a puck, pendant, clip-on or another portable form. The final form factor and specifications are not yet selected.
