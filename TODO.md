# Phonon pre-launch website

## Delivered outcomes

- [x] The site presents Maiyom Pvt. Ltd. and Phonon with exact naming and clear pre-launch positioning.
- [x] The product story explains the vision, mission, fragmented-app problem, and Phonon's intent-driven approach without invented metrics or unsupported claims.
- [x] The workflow section explains Understand, Plan, Act, and Verify in clear, grounded terms.
- [x] The use-case section covers planning, communication, reminders, and supported multi-step workflows without implying unsupported capabilities.
- [x] The trust section states that permission- or approval-dependent actions remain under the user's control and covers privacy/security principles.
- [x] The status and roadmap sections identify prototype-stage foundations and outstanding work in reliability, approvals, security hardening, and end-to-end validation.
- [x] The hardware concept is separated from the software product and labeled as exploratory rather than a confirmed specification.
- [x] A responsive pilot-interest / waitlist form is included with audience choices for pilot users, strategic partners, and feedback contributors.
- [x] The site has accessible section navigation, a mobile layout, a route manifest, and a visual identity consistent across sections.
- [ ] Connect the pilot-interest form to a live inbox or waitlist provider before public launch.
